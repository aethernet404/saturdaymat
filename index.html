About Jenny

Hi, I'm Jenny.

I'm a 200 hour certified yoga instructor. I created Saturday Mat because I wanted a space for yoga that feels approachable, supportive, and real.

You don't need to be flexible, experienced, or look a certain way to practice yoga. Just show up as you are.

FAQ

Good questions.

I've never done yoga. Can I come?

Absolutely. Saturday Mat is built for exactly that. Jenny walks you through the basics as class goes — you're never expected to already know what's next.

Do I need to bring a mat?

Please bring your own mat and a water bottle if you can. If you don't have one, no worries — limited mats are available, just let us know ahead of time.

Where do I park?

Free street parking on Cleveland Street and surrounding blocks. There's also a public parking garage at 349 N Cleveland St for $5 flat — just a short walk from the studio.

How early should I arrive?

Aim for 10–15 minutes early so you can get settled before class starts at 10:30.

What should I wear?

Anything you can move and breathe in — leggings, shorts, whatever you'd wear to work out.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancellation policy coming soon — for now, just email or text if your plans change.

Can I drop in?

Yes. No membership required to start — just reserve your spot for that Saturday and show up.
